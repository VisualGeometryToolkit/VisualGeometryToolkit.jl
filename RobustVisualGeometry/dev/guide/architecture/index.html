Architecture

Type Hierarchy

Block Coordinate Descent

AbstractBlockCoordinateMethod (VGC)          model block of block_coordinate_descent!
├── PosteriorIrlsMethod                      weighted-refit IRLS (Irls{K}, fit_residual_mixture, …)
└── (LocalOptimizationDoneRight)             joint-(θ,z) loss lifts (HQ{K}) and GNC (Gnc{S})

AbstractWeightPolicy (VGC)                   nuisance + weight updates driven by block_coordinate_descent!
└── PosteriorLikelihood                      Student-t + uniform mixture (one of several policies)

The generic block_coordinate_descent! loop (VisualGeometryCore robust/block_coordinate_descent.jl) dispatches on an AbstractBlockCoordinateMethod and a weight policy (AbstractWeightPolicy). PosteriorIrlsMethod is the weighted-refit plug-in: paired with PosteriorLikelihood and VisualGeometryCore's PosteriorWeightState it is the Student-t + uniform EM of fit_residual_mixture and fit_division_camera (and of LocalOptimizationDoneRight's StudentTUniform); Irls{K} runs the same method under a loss weight policy. The half-quadratic lifts and GNC are LocalOptimizationDoneRight's and bring their own methods and policies.

RANSAC Problems

AbstractRansacProblem{K}                     RANSAC problem interface, K = minimal sample size
├── LineFittingProblem
├── InhomLineFittingProblem
├── HandEyeProblem, RadialCameraProblem, RelposeBundleProblem
└── AbstractCspondProblem{K}                 Correspondence-based problems
    └── MinimalSolveProblem{M, K, T, ...}    the one parametric problem struct
        ├── HomographyProblem{T, ...}        alias: M = HomographyMat{T}, K = 4
        ├── FundMatProblem{T, ...}           alias: M = FundMat{T},       K = 7
        ├── EssentialProblem{T, ...}         alias: M = EssentialMat{T},  K = 5
        ├── AbsolutePoseProblem{...}         alias: M = Extrinsics3,      K = 3 (3D-2D)
        └── RadialHomographyProblem{T, K, ...}

HomographyProblem, FundMatProblem, EssentialProblem, AbsolutePoseProblem and RadialHomographyProblem are exported const aliases of the single parametric MinimalSolveProblem{M, K, T, ...} struct (defined in src/estimators/ransac/problems/cspond.jl). The model matrix type M selects per-model behaviour (codimension, minimal solver, Sampson whitening) via Holy traits like _model_codim(::Type{<:HomographyMat}) = 2.

RANSAC problems implement: minimal_sample_size, data_size, model_type, codimension, solve, residuals!, and optionally test_sample, test_model, test_consensus, refine!, constraint_with_jacobians and, for predictive scoring, provides_solver_covariance (the covariance itself is RVG's one solver_covariance, the constraint sandwich over an index set).

Scoring Functions

AbstractScoring (VGC)                        the scoring contract
├── MsacScoring{DG, T}, RansacScoring{DG, T} VGC's baseline scorers at a threshold
│                                            (MSAC is every find_*'s default)
└── StudentTUniformScoring{P, DG}            RansacScoringDoneRight's Student-t + uniform marginal
                                             (one downstream implementation)

The loop reads a scorer only through VisualGeometryCore's scoring contract: scoring_workspace(scoring, n, d_g) builds its scratch once, score! scores a model's residuals, inlier_count and consensus_order give its consensus set; the optional verbs (is_predictive, scored_model, scale_prior, outlier_halfwidth, inlier_threshold, check_workspace) default in VGC. The derived verbs score_model!, consensus_set, consensus_s2 and inlier_probabilities! are stated once in VGC and serve every scorer. See Scoring Functions.

The composed workspace

RansacWorkspace{K, M, T, W, L}             built once, reused across trials and calls
├── buf::ScoreBuffers{T, W}                residuals, per-point scores, consensus mask,
│                                          the scorer's workspace W, a freshness epoch
├── lo_ws::L                               lo_workspace(P, T, lo, n): for the core strategies a
│                                          LoBuffers (refine_workspace(P, n) + posterior buffers)
└── best_* / sample_indices                the incumbent (model, residuals, scores, mask, order)

RansacWorkspace(problem, scoring, local_optimization) (or RansacWorkspace(P, n_max, scoring, local_optimization; T)) composes it; the no-scoring form RansacWorkspace(P, n) builds the default MSAC scorer's. All dispatch on W and L is static.

Local Optimization (LO-RANSAC)

Local optimization refines promising hypotheses during the RANSAC loop through VisualGeometryCore's local-optimization contract: locally_optimize!(L, lo, problem, θ, cs::ConsensusSet, buf::ScoreBuffers, scoring) on the strategy's workspace L, returning lo_estimate(θ′, retcode; iterations, residuals_current). The core strategies are:

  • NoLocalOptimization — no local optimization anywhere; the default of ransac(problem, scoring). When selected, _finalize does not run LO either — the contract is "no LO, period."
  • Irls{K} — static IRLS on a VGC loss shape K at a frozen width. Irls{TLSShape}() with no width (it follows the scorer's inlier_threshold) is the default of every find_* pipeline ([pipelines.<class>] in configs/default.toml).

Every other strategy — the half-quadratic lifts HQ{K}, Gnc{S}, the Student-t + uniform IRLS StudentTUniform, the score-matched block-coordinate ascent, the Barron arms and the PoseLib refiners — is LocalOptimizationDoneRight's and plugs into the same contract.

Block-coordinate-descent extension interface

Downstream packages (e.g., BlobBoards) implement their own methods by extending a small set of public-declared interface methods: init_state!, update_model!, update_residuals!, update_nuisance!, update_weights!, post_iteration!, stopping_criterion (with stop_reason for a new criterion), block_coordinate_result. The loop block_coordinate_descent!(state, likelihood, method, problem, θ₀) dispatches on the method and calls through these hooks generically. (There is no trust-region hook: each model update carries its own damping.)

Workspace buffers

Problems that support LO-RANSAC implement refine!(ws, problem, θ, weights), the local optimization's one step from the current model. The generic refine! routes through the problem's refit_kind trait to _refine!(::NonlinLsq, …) — there is no fit-strategy marker. Each NonlinLsq refit is Levenberg–Marquardt on a minimal chart anchored at θ:

  • HomographyProblem: 8-dof sl(3) chart, whitened transfer residual with the Sampson shape Σ̃_{gᵢ} computed from the current θ and frozen for the solve; VisualGeometryCore's chart LM, FastLevenbergMarquardt's lmsolve! (workspace: ManifoldLmWorkspace{8}, refine_workspace(P, n), held in the local optimizer's workspace).
  • FundMatProblem: 7-dof Bartoli–Sturm chart, signed Sampson residual; the same core (workspace: ManifoldLmWorkspace{7}).
  • AbsolutePoseProblem: 6-dof left-perturbation chart (VGC LeftRigidManifold6), reprojection residual; the same core (workspace: ManifoldLmWorkspace{6}).
  • HandEyeProblem: 6-dof decoupled chart (VGC RigidExpManifold6, the chart of its constraint and covariance); the same core with a ForwardDiff Jacobian (workspace: ManifoldLmWorkspace{6}). Each hand-eye hypothesis is Tsai–Lenz fitted on its two motions (fit_hypothesis).

The posterior-weight buffers (posterior_w, posterior_inlier, posterior_sq_r) live on the core strategies' LoBuffers, beside the refit workspace, so a posterior-seeded local optimization is zero-allocation per hypothesis.

Dependency on VisualGeometryCore

RobustVisualGeometry imports geometry types, minimal solvers, the robust-loss shapes (the AbstractLossShape family: WelschShape, GemanMcclureShape, CauchyShape, SmoothTLSShape, TLSShape, HuberShape, GaussianShape, BarronShape) and the generic block-coordinate-descent loop (block_coordinate_descent!, robust/block_coordinate_descent.jl) from VGC. It extends VGC's test_model, test_sample, test_consensus, constraint_with_jacobians, solve and the loop's extension verbs with new methods. The loss shapes and the local-optimization trait vocabulary (ScaleInterface, optimization_path, …) are VGC's, exported by VGC; RVG re-exports nothing. The problem protocol (data_size, codimension, minimal_sample_size, residuals!, refine!, refine_workspace), the scoring and local-optimization contracts, the baseline scorers and the Student-t / posterior-weighting toolkit are VGC's too; RVG implements them for its problems and strategies.

Geometry-level model feasibility checks (test_model for HomographyMat, FundMat) live in VGC and dispatch on model type. RVG problem wrappers extract sample points and delegate to these VGC methods.

VisualGeometryCore (types, solvers, loss shapes, contracts, toolkit, MSAC/RANSAC scorers)
        ↓                                   ↓                         ↓
RobustVisualGeometry              RansacScoringDoneRight     LocalOptimizationDoneRight
(the RANSAC loop, problems,       (paper scorers; VGC only)  (paper strategies; VGC + RVG)
 NoLocalOptimization, Irls{K})

The user composes them: find_*(…; scoring = <a RansacScoringDoneRight scorer>, local_optimization = <a LocalOptimizationDoneRight strategy>). RobustVisualGeometry depends on neither downstream package.
