Model Manifolds and Uncertainty API

The minimal parameterizations the estimators refine on (the Manifold contract and its instances), first-order covariance transport and solver covariance, and the image-domain confidence regions that display it. Split from the Robust Estimation API page. See the Robust Estimation and Stereo Rigs and Uncertainty guides.

Model Manifolds

VisualGeometryToolkitCore.Manifold — Type
Manifold

Local coordinate system anchored at a model iterate. Contract:

  • tangent_size(ch)::Int — manifold dimension D;
  • model(ch) — the ambient model at the anchor (= retract(ch, 0));
  • retract(ch, v) — ambient model at local coordinates v::SVector{D};
  • recenter(ch, v) — the same manifold family re-anchored at retract(ch, v), with ONE normalization policy: a rotation part re-projected onto SO(3) (_reproject_so3), the homography chart's anchor its unit-Frobenius representative of the same model (a first-order retraction lets the scale drift); retract itself never normalizes;
  • retract_jacobian(ch, v = 0) — T(v) = ∂vec(retract(ch, v))/∂v (9 × D for 3×3 models): at the anchor by default, at any v for an optimizer that iterates in the manifold instead of re-anchoring. Named after the function it differentiates, as every <thing>_jacobian is: it is Ceres' Manifold::PlusJacobian and GTSAM's derivative of retract. The inverse form, if one is ever needed, is local_coordinates_jacobian (Ceres' MinusJacobian);
  • action_jacobian(ch, x, v = 0) — ∂(retract(ch, v)(x))/∂v, the manifold's action on a point or ray differentiated (at the anchor by default, at any v for an optimizer that iterates in the manifold): generically the chain rule of the model's own transform_deriv_params through T, closed form where a manifold has one;
  • local_coordinates(ch, target) — the inverse retraction (closed form for the rigid and lens manifolds, Gauss–Newton for the projective ones);
  • covariance transport through the retraction, first order: transform_covariance(ch, EuclideanManifold(ch), Σv) to the ambient space and back, or to another chart of the same model (see header).

NAMES, against the established libraries: retract and local_coordinates are GTSAM's retract / localCoordinates, which are Ceres' Manifold::Plus and Manifold::Minus. action_jacobian is the optional Jacobian GTSAM's transformFrom fills. The two constraint verbs have NO counterpart in either — Ceres and GTSAM both frame a problem as residuals of an explicit cost, while constraint_with_jacobians and constraint_jacobians are the IMPLICIT constraint g(θ, x) = 0 with Förstner's A = ∂g/∂θ and B = ∂g/∂x, which is what an implicit-function covariance and a Gauss-Helmert step need.

A manifold is the MODEL's (minimal_manifold(model)), and the free/fixed choice a fitter makes is a manifold operation (restrict) — so a residual composed with a manifold is differentiated once, here, not per consumer.

VisualGeometryToolkitCore.action_jacobian — Method
action_jacobian(ch::Manifold, x) -> SMatrix
action_jacobian(ch::Manifold, x, v) -> SMatrix

The Jacobian of the manifold's ACTION on x — ∂(retract(ch, v)(x))/∂v at v = 0, or at the chart coordinates v — what every residual through a posed or parameterized model needs. Generic: the chain rule of the model's own parameter derivative (transform_deriv_params(retract(ch, v), x), ∂model(x)/∂ambient) through the retraction differential (retract_jacobian(ch, v), ∂ambient/∂v). A manifold with a closed form overrides it (RigidManifold6: [−4·skew(R·x) I₃] at the anchor); every override is pinned against this default in the manifold tests.

VisualGeometryToolkitCore.retract_jacobian — Function
retract_jacobian(ch::Manifold) -> SMatrix
retract_jacobian(ch::Manifold, v::StaticVector) -> SMatrix

The RETRACTION DIFFERENTIAL: ∂vec(retract(ch, v))/∂v, the ambient model's derivative with respect to the chart coordinates — at the anchor (v = 0) in the one-argument form, at any v for an optimizer that iterates in the manifold instead of re-anchoring.

Named after the function it differentiates, as every <thing>_jacobian is. Against the established libraries it is Ceres' Manifold::PlusJacobian and GTSAM's derivative of retract; the inverse form, if one is ever needed, is local_coordinates_jacobian (Ceres' MinusJacobian).

This is the ∂ambient/∂v factor of action_jacobian, which chains it with the model's own transform_deriv_params. The default for a chart that does not override it is _retract_jacobian_fd, ForwardDiff through retract — exact to first order with no hand-derivation risk, and every closed-form override is pinned against it in the manifold contract tests. A linear chart (LensManifold) returns the identity. The default needs only retract and the model's _ambient_coordinates.

Public, not exported; VisualGeometryToolkitEstimation builds its LM and covariance steps on it.

VisualGeometryToolkitCore.transform_covariance — Method
transform_covariance(from::Manifold, to::Manifold, Σ) -> Matrix

A covariance in from's coordinates re-expressed in to's, the two charts anchored at the same model and sharing its ambient vectorization, to first order: through the ambient space, T_to⁺·(T_from·Σ·T_fromᵀ)·T_to⁺ᵀ (T the charts' retract_jacobians). The ambient space is a chart as well, EuclideanManifold(ch): TO it the covariance is pushed, T·Σ·Tᵀ; FROM it pulled, T⁺·Σ·T⁺ᵀ (a gauge-deficient chart projects its gauge out). Exact where to is full rank. A rigid pose's covariance between the MRP (RigidManifold6), left-perturbation (LeftRigidManifold6) and decoupled (RigidExpManifold6) charts, for instance — the MRP's factor 4 at the anchor and the translation coupling done by the charts themselves, not by hand. A change of coordinates at one model, not a parallel transport between points.

GTSAM equivalent: TransformCovariance<T> (a group-frame change), and the Local/Retract Jacobians a chart change composes.

VisualGeometryToolkitCore.LeftRigidManifold6 — Type
LeftRigidManifold6(R, t) <: Manifold
LeftRigidManifold6(pose::EuclideanMap{3})

The LEFT-perturbation pose chart, RigidChart{ExpParam, CarriedT}: coordinates v = (δω₁..₃, δt₁..₃) act on the whole map, x ↦ exp([δω]×)·(R·x + t) + δt, so retract(ch, v) is (exp_so3(δω)·R, exp_so3(δω)·t + δt).

It is the perturbation constraint_with_jacobians differentiates for a pose (G_θ = Jπ·[−[X_cam]× I₃]): action_jacobian(ch, x) = [−[R·x + t]× I₃], so a PnP refit — and the PnP lift, which is the refit's rows at v = 0 — that solves in these coordinates re-anchors (recenter) with exactly the perturbation its Jacobian assumed. local_coordinates is the closed-form inverse (δω = log_so3(R·R₀ᵀ), δt = t − exp_so3(δω)·t₀). Float-typed storage (SMatrix{3,3,T}, SVector{3,T}). See RigidChart.

VisualGeometryToolkitCore.RigidChart — Type
RigidChart{P <: RotationParam, C <: TranslationCoupling, Rot, Tr} <: Manifold

The 6-dof rigid pose chart family, anchored at (R::Rot, t::Tr): coordinates v = (ω₁..₃, τ₁..₃) act as R(v) = ΔR(ω)·R and t(v) = t + τ (FreeT) or ΔR(ω)·t + τ (CarriedT), ΔR the increment of P (MRPParam, ExpParam). RigidExpManifold6(R, t) <: Manifold
RigidExpManifold6(pose::EuclideanMap{3})

The DECOUPLED pose chart, RigidChart{ExpParam, FreeT}: R(v) = exp_so3(δω)·R, t(v) = t + δt. Unlike LeftRigidManifold6 the rotation does not carry the translation; unlike RigidManifold6 the rotation coordinate is the rotation vector (radians), not the MRP. VisualGeometryToolkitCore.RigidManifold6 — Type
RigidManifold6(R, t) <: Manifold

The MRP rigid pose chart, RigidChart{MRPParam, FreeT}: R(v) = RotMatrix(MRP(ω))·R, t(v) = t + τ. Parametric in the anchor's storage types (a RotMatrix or an SMatrix, any static 3-vector), which recenter keeps — the rigid-pose retraction of P3P, the relpose-bundle pose block and BlobBoards' bundle. VisualGeometryToolkitCore.SphereManifold — Type
SphereManifold(q::StaticVector{N}, embed = identity) <: Manifold

The unit sphere at q (normalized on construction): coordinates v ∈ R^{N−1} along an orthonormal basis B of q's complement — the last N − 1 columns of the Householder reflection taking e₁ to ∓q, built from q + sign(q₁)·e₁ so it is stable for every q — and retract(ch, v) = embed(normalize(q + B·v)).

embed turns the unit vector into the model: the identity for a bare vector, a RadialCamera with its centre for minimal_manifold(::RadialCamera). The ambient space is the vector's: retract_jacobian(ch, v) is (I − ŷŷᵀ)·B/‖q + B·v‖ (N × (N − 1), B at the anchor). local_coordinates(ch, y) is closed form and invariant to the scale and sign of y: Bᵀy/(qᵀy). recenter re-anchors at the retracted point, with a new basis.

VisualGeometryToolkitCore.Line2DManifold — Type
Line2DManifold(l::Line2D) <: Manifold

The 2-dof chart of a 2D line n·x + d = 0 (|n| = 1): v = (φ, δd) rotates the normal by φ and moves the offset, retract(ch, v) = Line2D(R(φ)·n, d + δd). The ambient space is the line's coefficients (n₁, n₂, d), retract_jacobian [R(φ)·t 0; 0 1] with t = (−n₂, n₁) the line's direction. A line acts on a point by its signed distance (constraint_with_jacobians(l, x)), so action_jacobian(ch, x) = (t·x, 1) — the point-on-line constraint's chart row. local_coordinates is closed form and reads (n, d) and (−n, −d) as the same line. minimal_manifold(l::Line2D) is this chart.

VisualGeometryToolkitCore.constraint_jacobians — Method
constraint_jacobians(ch::Manifold, v, cs) -> (∂_v g, ∂_x g)

The Jacobians of manifold_constraints at chart coordinates v: with respect to v (G_θ·retract_jacobian(ch, v), dg·k × D) and to the measurements (block diagonal, dg·k × nₓk, each pair's G_x over (x₁, y₁, x₂, y₂), or over the image point of a pose correspondence, nₓ = 2). At v = 0 these are the two factors of the implicit-function covariance (implicit_covariance); at v ≠ 0, the Newton matrix of a chart-Newton solve that iterates in the manifold.

NO Ceres or GTSAM counterpart: both frame a problem as residuals of an EXPLICIT cost, whereas this is the IMPLICIT constraint g(θ, x) = 0 in Förstner's form — A = ∂g/∂θ, B = ∂g/∂x — which is what an implicit-function covariance and a Gauss-Helmert step consume. The value-and-Jacobians sibling is constraint_with_jacobians.

constraint_with_jacobians(l::Line2D, x::StaticVector{2}) -> (g, G_x::SVector{2}, G_θ::SVector{3})

The point-on-line constraint g = n·x + d — the point's signed distance (the normal is unit) — with G_x = n and G_θ = ∂g/∂(n₁, n₂, d) = (x₁, x₂, 1), the line's coefficients (Line2DManifold's ambient space). Single-point data: the covariance machinery takes a vector of points for a line chart.

VisualGeometryToolkitCore.implicit_covariance — Method
implicit_covariance(ch::Manifold, cs; Σx = nothing) -> Matrix (D×D) or nothing
implicit_covariance(block, indices; noise_weighted = false) -> Matrix or nothing
implicit_covariance(A::AbstractMatrix, B::AbstractMatrix; Σx = nothing) -> Matrix or nothing

The first-order covariance of the solution v(x) of an implicitly defined estimation problem (Haralick's framing): g(v, x) = 0 for a square system (a minimal solve), ∂_v(½‖W₀^{½}·g‖²) = 0 — least-squares stationarity of the whitened constraints — for an overdetermined one. With A = ∂_v g, B = ∂_x g and the estimator's own isotropic whitening W₀ = blockdiag((BᵢBᵢᵀ)⁻¹) over the correspondences' row blocks,

Σ̃_v = (AᵀW₀A)⁻¹ · AᵀW₀·(B·Σx·Bᵀ)·W₀A · (AᵀW₀A)⁻¹,

which is A⁻¹·B·Σx·Bᵀ·A⁻ᵀ when A is square (the whitening cancels — the implicit-function theorem proper) and (AᵀW₀A)⁻¹ for unit isotropic Σx. Unit scale: Σx defaults to unit isotropic measurement noise; the physical covariance is s²·Σ̃_v with s the consumer's. Invariant to any invertible per-correspondence recombination of the constraint rows (the algebraic and the transfer forms of a homography give the same Σ̃_v).

  • Chart first: the constraints of the correspondences cs (u₁ => u₂ pairs; X => u for a pose chart; single points for a line) differentiated at the anchor of ch (constraint_jacobians), in the coordinates of ch, which must be anchored at the estimate. Σx: one covariance shape per correspondence (4×4 over (x₁, y₁, x₂, y₂); 2×2 for a pose's or a line's point), in the coordinates of cs — data mapped to another frame needs its noise transported there (the map's transform_deriv) before a pixel s² means anything. A minimal sample (k·d_g = D) takes the square solve.
  • Blocks, never materializing B (O(D²) memory, for large inlier sets): block(i) returns (Aᵢ, Bᵢ) or (Aᵢ, Bᵢ, Σxᵢ) for each i in indices — the correspondence's rows of ∂_v g and ∂_x g (a StaticVector is one row) and its covariance shape (unit isotropic when absent). Function first, as in map(f, xs). With noise_weighted = true the estimator is instead the one weighted by each block's OWN noise, W₀ᵢ = (BᵢΣxᵢBᵢᵀ)⁻¹ (the Σxᵢ-whitened residuals a scorer uses), whose covariance is (AᵀW₀A)⁻¹ — Σxᵢ folded into Bᵢ as Bᵢ·chol(Σxᵢ).L. The default sandwiches Σxᵢ around the isotropically weighted estimator: a different estimator, a different covariance whenever the Σxᵢ are not isotropic.
  • Dense square factors: A = ∂_v g (D×D) and B = ∂_x g (D×nₓk) of a square system g(v, x) = 0 whose rows are not per-correspondence constraints — e.g. the stationarity conditions of a staged estimator, one row block per stage — gives A⁻¹·B·Σx·Bᵀ·A⁻ᵀ directly. Σx: one covariance shape per column block of B (nₓ columns each, k blocks); unit when absent. The minimal-sample path of the chart form above is this method.

nothing when the system is singular (A for a square one, AᵀW₀A otherwise — a degenerate sample, the same the solvers' own gates reject, or ill-posed geometry) or when there are no blocks; a block whose Bᵢ is degenerate (whitening_factor's rule) drops out, as it does from the estimator.

GTSAM equivalent: Marginals::jointMarginalCovariance at the optimum, when the factor rows are correctly whitened. This one also covers the square minimal solve and the sandwich for a mismatched weighting.

VisualGeometryToolkitCore.local_coordinates — Method
local_coordinates(ch::Manifold, Mtarget; iters=25, tol=1e-13)
    -> SVector{tangent_size(ch)}

Inverse retraction ("manifold log"): the local coordinates v such that retract(ch, v) ∝ Mtarget (projective alignment — scale AND sign of the target are matched to the manifold family at each Gauss–Newton step, so any solver normalization convention may be supplied). This is the harness primitive for expressing an estimate IN THE MANIFOLD RECENTERED AT TRUTH.

Gauge-deficient manifolds (e.g. EssentialManifold6, near-essential FundMatManifold7 anchors) resolve the step through pinv, projecting the gauge direction out — the returned coordinates are the minimum-norm representative. Converges quadratically for targets in the manifold's injectivity neighborhood (MC-perturbation scale); for far targets check the residual.

VisualGeometryToolkitCore.LineConfidenceBand — Type
LineConfidenceBand

Confidence region of an uncertain homogeneous 2D line: nominal line l, line covariance Σl (3×3, from pushing a model covariance through the line construction), and squared Mahalanobis level k2. Region: {x : (x̃ᵀl)² ≤ k2·x̃ᵀΣl x̃}; boundary conic C = l·lᵀ − k2·Σl. Plot with lines!(ax, band, rect) (Makie layer) or sample the boundary with band_boundary.

band_boundary(b::LineConfidenceBand, rect::HyperRectangle{2}; n=201)
    -> (upper::Vector{Point2{Float64}}, lower::Vector{Point2{Float64}})

Sample the two boundary branches of the band inside rect, as offsets along the nominal line's normal over n stations of the clipped nominal segment. Stations where the band is UNBOUNDED (the near-degeneracy blow-up: the normal-section quadratic loses its positive curvature or its real roots) emit (NaN, NaN) so polylines break there instead of lying. VisualGeometryToolkitCore.confidence_ellipse — Method
confidence_ellipse(μ, Σ; level=0.95) -> Ellipse

The level confidence ellipse of a 2D Gaussian N(μ, Σ): {x : (x−μ)ᵀ Σ⁻¹ (x−μ) = χ²₂(level)}, as the standard Ellipse primitive (so it plots through the existing poly!/convert_arguments layer).

It is Ellipse(μ, Symmetric(χ²₂(level)·Σ)), so a Σ that is not positive definite (singular, indefinite, or NaN) is refused with that constructor's ArgumentError, never clamped into a degenerate ellipse.
