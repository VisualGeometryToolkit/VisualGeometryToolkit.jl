The Framed Imaging Chain

The imaging chain of a central camera, as framed stages with checked junctions (since 0.10):

ViewFrame{id} ─ ray-frame projection → ray frame (Gnomonic/Equidistant/…)
              ─ lens distortion      → DistortedFrame{id}
              ─ framed K-affine      → ImageFrame{id}

imaging_chain mints the fully-typed composite {ViewFrame{id} → ImageFrame{id}}; the frame-free imaging_transform is the same map without frames, AffineMap(K) ∘ lens with the lens applied to the ray. The scalar lens math is never reimplemented: the framed stages wrap the lens contract's maps.

One retina, many frames

Every central camera has a spherical retina — the sphere S² of rays through the center of projection (the manifold-level object of the Geyer–Daniilidis unified imaging model). What differs between camera models is the frame their distortion is expressed in — a 2D coordinate system on the retina, in differential-geometry terms a chart of the sphere — so frames, not retinas, are the type currency of the chain. The perspective plane is not a different manifold: it is the gnomonic chart of the sphere, degenerate at 90° and blind to Z ≤ 0 rays.

The RetinalFrame{id} family (complete since 0.13) shares the azimuth and differs in the radial profile, each with its own honest domain:

frameradial profiledomainnote
GnomonicFramer = tan θθ ∈ [0, π/2)gnomonic; calibrated coordinates x̂ = π(K⁻¹ũ)
EquidistantFramer = θθ ∈ [0, π)the omnidirectional models' defining frame
StereographicFramer = 2·tan(θ/2)θ ∈ [0, π)conformal — the only angle-preserving member
EquisolidFramer = 2·sin(θ/2)θ ∈ [0, π]equal-area; the only frame reaching the antipode
OrthographicFramer = sin θθ ∈ [0, π/2] onlyfolds at the equator

Two more frames complete the vocabulary: DistortedFrame{id} — the post-lens, pre-K frame forward distortion lands on (defined by the lens map's image, deliberately not a RetinalFrame) — and ImageFrame{id}, the canonical pixel frame.

Stage 1: the ray-frame projection family

"Perspective precedes lens" is a property of perspective-plane models, not a law of the chain. Stage 1 is therefore a family — one framed projection per ray frame: FramedPerspective, FramedEquidistantProjection, FramedStereographicProjection, FramedEquisolidProjection, FramedOrthographicProjection. Each is a fiber collapse (3D ray → 2D ray-frame point) and deliberately has no inv — the right-inverses are the sections unproject and backproject, which select one point per fiber via a depth.

distortion_frame(lens) names which frame a lens model's distortion is defined in, and hence which projection stage 1 must be: for a Lens it is its projection's frame (projection_frame) — GnomonicFrame for the perspective projection, EquidistantFrame for the equidistant one (Kannala–Brandt's radial factor, COLMAP's thin-prism fisheye), and so on; a LiftLens is read on the gnomonic frame. A parametric projection (DoubleSphere, Unified, ExtendedUnified) lands on a frame its coefficients define, which no tag can name: imaging_chain serves such a lens through one fused FramedLensProjection, and the split mints refuse it (ArgumentError).

Stages 2–3: distortion and calibration

distort_stage(lens) mints the framed forward distortion {distortion_frame(lens){id} → DistortedFrame{id}} as a FramedDistort; undistort_stage is its frames-swapped inv (FramedUndistort). calibration_stage(K) mints the framed affine action of the intrinsics ({DistortedFrame{id} → ImageFrame{id}}) as a FramedAffine.

Direction checking is the point: composing an undistort stage after another undistort stage, or after the ray projection, is a junction mismatch and throws a MethodError — distort-vs-undistort direction is what the frame tags check, view identity or not.

The collapse rule

When a lens's distortion is the identity in its defining frame (Pinhole on the gnomonic GnomonicFrame; the pure-projection fisheyes on theirs), there is no distortion stage: the chain collapses the ray frame onto the calibration stage — K consumes the ray frame directly — rather than inserting a manufactured identity stage. distort_stage errors for such lenses; the collapse is applied by imaging_chain automatically.

The anonymous view id

The id axis is optional. The reserved symbol :_ is the designated anonymous view identity and every stage mint defaults to it — frame kind then still does the junction checking, so a lone CameraModel with no view identity gets direction-checked stages. Rig-derived chains thread real identities:

a, b = ids(rig)
chain₁ = imaging_chain(model(source(rig)); id = a)   # {ViewFrame{a} → ImageFrame{a}}

:_ is a real label, not a wildcard — the UnlabeledFrame rules are unchanged.

Composition and FramedChain

∘ between framed stages is junction-checked under the standing wildcard truth table (same five-method statement as EuclideanMap) and produces a flat FramedChain, whose callable unrolls at compile time. The vararg FramedChain(stages...) constructor checks every inner junction and names the first mismatch. inv inverts and reverses the stages — and is therefore a MethodError for chains containing a ray projection.

Numerically, imaging_chain matches imaging_transform: bit-identical for perspective-plane lenses, equal to round-off for the other frames. Both work in the model's defining frame; neither hops through the gnomonic plane.
