The composition evaluator

The composition evaluator measures a RANSAC scorer (the selection step) composed with a local optimizer (LO, the refinement step) on stored pool pairs. It lives in the Evaluation module, in src/evaluation/engine/.

An evaluated method is called an arm. Each arm is a pair of values that implement VisualGeometryToolkitCore's contracts:

  • a scoring, which implements score!, select_on_grid! and with_width;
  • a local optimization, whose RefitFamily routes it to one of two paths. NativeRefit runs locally_optimize! inside VisualGeometryToolkitEstimation's RANSAC loop. ExternalRefit hands the model to a third-party refiner that maps a model to a model (refine_external!).

The evaluator holds no scorer or optimizer of its own. A project that evaluates particular scorers or optimizers depends on their packages and supplies their values.

The pieces

PieceWhat it does
PairCtx, build_pair_ctxbuilds, once per pool pair, the pool-frame problem, the calibrations, the ground-truth (GT) pose and the refit-scoring template that every refit shares
refit_imc!, guarded_refitthe one refit layer: one refit of a seed model, with its pose error, its wall time and a fallback to the seed
LoRefitSpec, apply_lo_refitsturns a selection sweep into post-LO error matrices (pairs by grid columns)
stream_selectionsstreams the scenes: it stages each scene, runs select_on_grid! per grid, then one apply_lo_refits
SampleBudgetMethodSpec, MsacSpec, evaluate_sample_budgetthe sample-budget engine: running-best selectors and LO checkpoints along each pair's hypothesis stream
live_runs, live_rowend-to-end ransac runs on pool pairs, with a problem the caller builds per pair
ComposedSpec, SoloSpec, Roster, deployed_arm_settings, audit_operating_pointsa paper's arms, and the gated readers of their tuned operating points

"MSAC" is the M-estimator sample consensus score, the truncated-quadratic baseline scorer of VisualGeometryToolkitCore.

Widths

Widths are VisualGeometryToolkitCore width values. Each path receives its width in the form it consumes:

  • A native refit is seeded under the scoring template rebuilt at its own outlier half-width, with_width(scoring, OutlierHalfwidth(a)).
  • An absolute width reaches the strategy as with_width(lo, fixed_width(lo, s2)).
  • An external refiner receives LossWidth(w), or FixedS2(s2) when the caller supplies the scale.

A refit, step by step

The snippet below shows the shape of one refit. It needs a pool pair, a scoring template with an outlier half-width, a local optimization and a seed model, which come from a scoring pool and from the packages that implement the scorer and the optimizer.

using VisualGeometryToolkitEval.Evaluation: build_pair_ctx, refit_imc!, problem_type

P = problem_type(:E)                              # the essential-matrix problem type
ctx = build_pair_ctx(P, pair; scoring = template)
imc, lo_ns = refit_imc!(ctx, lo, seed, nat)       # nat: the width of lo on the axis it consumes

imc is the pose error of the refit model in degrees, max(ΔR, Δt) over the best decomposition, as the Image Matching Challenge (IMC) defines it. lo_ns is the optimizer's wall time in nanoseconds. consumed_axis and consumed_native give the width that lo consumes.
